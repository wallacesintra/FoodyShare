# FoodyCare

app that has assist in food donation

## objectives

* manage food wastage
* feed the homeless

## features

* donate
* collect
* chat
* Locate

## parties

### donors

they can include:

* hotels
* food factory
* caterers
* events/parties
* anyone

if they have food to donate, they indicate on the app
alert will be sent to the other parties

### donation receivers

they can include:

* childrens home
* NGOs
* slums
* needy
* Non formal educational centres
* spots where homeless stays

## user

### Properties

* name
* address
* contact
* bio
* type(donor/receiver)
